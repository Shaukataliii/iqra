# Iqra

**Read consistently. Capture insights. Grow through what you read.**

Iqra is an Android app that helps you build a consistent reading habit while making it easier to remember and use what you learn. Instead of only tracking pages, Iqra encourages active reading through goals, notes, questions, and progress tracking.

Whether you're reading physical books, PDFs, textbooks, Islamic literature, or novels, Iqra helps you stay consistent and organized.

## Features

* 📚 Track multiple books simultaneously
* ⏱️ Set daily reading goals
* 📝 Write notes and questions while reading
* 📖 Resume from your last reading position
* 📅 Visual reading calendar and progress tracking
* 🔔 Gentle reading reminders
* 🌍 Works with books in any language

## Why Iqra?

Many people start books with enthusiasm but struggle to stay consistent. Even after finishing a book, valuable ideas are often forgotten.

Iqra aims to solve both problems by helping you:

* Build a sustainable reading habit.
* Capture important ideas as you read.
* Revisit your notes whenever you need them.
* Turn reading into long-term personal growth.
